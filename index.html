How I lead

I joined my first scale-up as its 40th employee and its first operations hire. Since then I have learned that most growth problems are really clarity problems: nobody knows which three numbers matter this quarter, or who owns them.

My approach is simple and unglamorous — a small set of shared metrics, a weekly operating rhythm, and leaders trusted to make decisions close to the customer.

  • Built four executive teams from scratch
  • Led two cross-border acquisitions and integrations
  • Non-executive director at a Nasdaq-listed payments company
  • Mentor with the Women in Operations Network
